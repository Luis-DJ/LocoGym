# Privacy Policy for LocoGym

**Effective date: 1 September 2026**

LocoGym is an offline workout-recording application developed by **Quiet Robot Studio**.

## Our privacy principle

LocoGym is designed around a simple promise:

**No account. No cloud. No nonsense.**

LocoGym does not require an account and does not transmit your workout information to Quiet Robot Studio or to any cloud service.

## Information stored by LocoGym

LocoGym allows you to record information such as:

- Workout templates and exercises
- Weights, repetitions and sets
- Rest periods
- Workout dates and completion status
- Workout history and performance statistics

This information is stored locally on your device.

## Information we collect

Quiet Robot Studio does not collect, receive or store your personal information or workout data.

LocoGym does not include advertising, analytics or user-tracking services.

## Internet access 

LocoGym does not require Internet access for its normal operation and does not upload your workout information to a server.

Google Play and the Android operating system may independently process information relating to downloading, installing, updating, security and diagnostics. Such processing is governed by Google’s own privacy policies.

## Importing and exporting information

LocoGym allows you to import workout templates and export workout history.

Imported information is read from a file selected by you. Exported information is saved or shared only with the location or application that you choose. Quiet Robot Studio does not receive these files.

You are responsible for protecting any exported files and for choosing where they are stored or shared.

## Device features

LocoGym may use device features such as sound, vibration and notifications to provide rest-timer alerts. These features do not transmit workout data to Quiet Robot Studio.

## Data sharing

Quiet Robot Studio does not sell, rent or share your workout information with third parties because it does not receive that information.

## Data retention and deletion

Your LocoGym information remains on your device until you:

- Delete workout history or other records using the available app controls;
- Clear LocoGym’s storage through Android settings; or
- Uninstall the application.

Because Quiet Robot Studio does not hold a copy of your information, we cannot recover information deleted from your device.

## Health and fitness notice

LocoGym is a personal workout-recording tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare or fitness professional.

Consult an appropriate professional before beginning or changing an exercise programme, particularly if you have a medical condition, injury or concern about exercising safely.

## Children

LocoGym is intended for users aged 18 years and over. It is not designed for children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if LocoGym’s features or data practices change. The effective date shown above will be updated when changes are made.

If LocoGym later introduces optional cloud synchronisation, accounts, analytics or other online services, this policy and the relevant in-app disclosures will be updated before those features are released.
